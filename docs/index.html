Free, open source browser extension

Every link worth keeping, in one tidy place.

Save Link Extension organises the pages you want to come back to into colourful categories, and keeps them in sync across browsers through a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive.

Small tool, everything you reach for.

No account is needed to start. Your links work offline, right in the browser.

The same links on every browser

Sign in with Google on your laptop, your work PC and any other Chromium browser. Changes sync automatically, and existing links are merged when you sign in.

Colourful categories

Group links by topic, pick a colour for each group and drag to reorder them.

Right-click to save

Save the page you are on straight into a category from the context menu.

Instant search

Find any link by its address or description as you type.

Done checkboxes

Tick off what you have read, watched or finished.

Edit in place

Change a link or category right in the list, with no extra forms.

One click to open or copy

Open a link in a new tab or copy its address straight away.

Export and import

Back up everything to a JSON file and restore it whenever you like.

Your data lives in your Drive, not on our server.

There is no server of ours in the middle. The extension talks directly to Google, so the only copy in the cloud is a sheet in your own account.

Developer access: none. No analytics, no ads, and no copy of your links on any other server.

What Google will ask you to allow, and why.

These permissions are requested only if you choose to sign in. Without signing in, the extension keeps your links in your browser.

openid email profile

Your name, email and profile photo

Shown in the popup so you can see which account is syncing, and used to make sure sync stays on that account.

drive.file

Only the file the extension creates

Lets the extension create and update its sheet, “Link Inventory Extension [Dont delete]”. It cannot see or change your other Drive files.

Full details are in the privacy policy.

Install in under a minute.

  1. DownloadDownload the ZIP and unzip it.
  2. Open extensionsGo to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Load itClick “Load unpacked” and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Sign in (optional)Click “Sign In” in the popup to start syncing.
